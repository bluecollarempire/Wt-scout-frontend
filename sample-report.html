General education about gold prospecting — not a finding about your location. A little taste of the guide.

How Gold Moves With Water

Gold is heavy. It drops out wherever water slows: inside bends, behind boulders, at gradient breaks, where two creeks meet. Learn to read the water and you learn to read the ground.

Reading a Pan

A pan tells the truth in minutes. A few colors means you’re in the right drainage; no color after several pans means move on. Pan the gravels at the bedrock contact first — that’s where the heavies settle.

What a Detector Can and Can’t Do

A gold detector finds metal near the surface — and depth is everything. Shallow targets are in easy reach; deep ones may be beyond your machine. When you reach a target’s lat/long, pull out the metal detector and check first — if nothing sounds, it could be too deep for your detector. Detect and dig if you have to, to prove your target.

Claim Basics

A mining claim is a right to mine, not ownership of the land. Claims are marked on your report’s map with their lat/long. Always verify current status with the field office before you stake, detect or dig.
